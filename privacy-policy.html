Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ogarsmarket, operating the OGARS Market platform, collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when people visit or use the marketplace.

Last updated: 22 November 2026
Important: This is OGARS Market's published privacy notice. It describes the platform's intended handling of information and should be read together with the Terms & Conditions. It does not replace any registration, security, operational or other compliance steps required by Ghanaian law.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

For this notice, the platform operator is Ogarsmarket (“OGARS”, “we”, “us” or “our”). We determine why and how personal information is used in connection with the OGARS Market website, subject to applicable law.

Contact: abuaogarjohn@gmail.com · 0596111913 (phone/WhatsApp).

We have not stated a postal or registered office address in this notice because an official business address has not been provided.

2. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on how you use OGARS Market. It may include:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, account identifiers and information you provide when registering or managing an account.
  • Seller and advert information: business or seller details, advert titles, descriptions, category, location, price or other listing details you submit.
  • Contact information in adverts: telephone number, WhatsApp number, website address and other contact details you choose to publish.
  • Content and files: product photographs, videos, profile images, documents, messages or other material you choose to upload or submit, where those features are available.
  • Platform and technical information: information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website, which may include device, browser, log, diagnostic and network information made available by the services used to operate the platform.
  • Support and safety information: correspondence with OGARS, reports, reviews, complaints, evidence and records relating to platform safety or account administration.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary and the platform has clearly requested it through an appropriate process.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information for purposes connected with operating and improving OGARS Market, including to:

  • create, authenticate and manage user accounts;
  • publish and display adverts and seller profiles as requested by users;
  • allow buyers and sellers to contact one another using details a user chooses to publish;
  • provide customer support and respond to enquiries;
  • review reports, investigate suspected fraud, enforce platform rules and protect users;
  • administer seller verification and, when implemented, seller reviews and accountability processes;
  • maintain website functionality, security, backups and troubleshooting;
  • meet legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We aim to use information only where it is relevant and necessary for the stated purpose or another lawful basis under applicable law.

4. What other users can see

OGARS Market is a marketplace. Information you choose to include in a public advert or public-facing seller profile may be visible to visitors, including people who do not have an account. This can include your advert content, photographs, stated location and contact details such as a phone or WhatsApp number.

Do not publish your home address, identity documents, financial credentials or other information you do not want made public. Once information is public, other people may copy, save or share it. Removing an advert from OGARS may not remove copies already made by others or content cached elsewhere.

5. When we share information

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. We may share information only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, including:

  • With other users and the public: information you choose to publish in adverts or profiles, and information needed for buyer-seller contact.
  • With service providers: technology providers that help host, store, secure, maintain or operate the platform. OGARS currently uses Supabase services for certain database, authentication and file-storage functions.
  • For safety and legal reasons: where disclosure is required by law, a lawful request, court process or is reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, enforce our terms or protect rights and safety.
  • With your direction: where you ask us to share information or otherwise give a valid instruction or consent.

Service providers should process information only for authorised services and subject to appropriate confidentiality and security arrangements. Some providers may process information in locations outside Ghana; the applicable safeguards and transfer requirements must be assessed for the services in use.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The website and its service providers may use browser storage, session technologies or similar technical mechanisms to support functions such as account sign-in, security, preferences and reliable operation. The exact technologies may change as the platform develops. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings, but disabling certain storage may affect sign-in or website functionality.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to operate accounts and adverts, provide support, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the circumstances.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention requirements, legitimate record-keeping needs and technical backup cycles.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep passwords and account credentials confidential and contact us promptly if they suspect unauthorised access to their account.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request information about the personal data we hold about you, request access to it, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, or request deletion where appropriate. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before acting. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

10. Children's privacy

OGARS Market is a general marketplace and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authority or safeguards. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps.

11. Third-party websites and services

Adverts or platform pages may contain links to websites, payment services, social media or other third-party services. Those services are operated independently and have their own privacy practices. OGARS is not responsible for the privacy or security practices of third-party websites. Please review their privacy notices before providing information to them.

12. Future features and protected transactions

OGARS is considering optional protected transactions and related payment, inspection and dispute-resolution features. These features are not represented by this notice as currently available. If and when they are introduced, OGARS will provide additional information about the data required, payment service providers, transaction records, dispute evidence and applicable terms before users are asked to use them.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this notice as the platform, our services or applicable legal requirements change. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Where a change materially affects users, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to their attention.
